# Context: *Lakers versus Celtics and the NBA Playoffs* (1989)

Reference material for contributors who want to match the look, feel and pacing of the game NtBA
is inspired by. This folder holds **links, our own notes and a small set of reference
screenshots**. The screenshots are copyrighted by EA and kept for reference only; nothing in this
folder may be copied into the game (the project's rule is *own everything*). Prefer linking to
further screenshots over adding more, and put your own measurements and observations in notes.

## Facts worth knowing (from the sources above)

- Electronic Arts, MS-DOS in 1989, Sega Genesis in 1990 (Genesis uses 1990 playoff rosters).
- First NBA-endorsed game and one of the first to feature multiple real stars and teams.
- Eight playoff teams plus two All-Star teams.
- TV-style starting-lineup introduction before the opening tip; playoff mode with an in-studio
  halftime show of other playoff results.
- Official rules: 24-second shot clock, 10-second backcourt, 5-second inbound, overtime,
  travelling, offensive/defensive fouls, out of bounds.
- Offence: dribble in any direction, pass, pump-fake, shoot. The 1989 review criticised weak
  sound and the lack of crowd noise — NtBA can do better.
