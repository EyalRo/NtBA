## NtBA in the game

The game runs at 320x200 and is shown at 4:3, scaled with nearest-neighbour so the pixels stay crisp.

**Side view** — the sideline camera used for the backcourt and fast breaks:

![Side view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/EyalRo/NtBA/images/game-side-view.png)

**End-on view** — the half-court camera looking at the basket:

![End-on view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/EyalRo/NtBA/images/game-end-view.png)

**Attacking the hoop:**

![Going for a shot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/EyalRo/NtBA/images/game-shot.png)
