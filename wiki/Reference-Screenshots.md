# Reference screenshots

Reference-only screenshots of the 1989 DOS and 1990 Genesis versions of *Lakers versus Celtics and
the NBA Playoffs* (© Electronic Arts). They are here so contributors can study the look of the
original; they are **not** covered by this project's GPL license and **must not** be used as game
assets (NtBA's art is original). If a rights holder asks, they will be removed.

| File | Version | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`dos-ega-free-throw-lineup.webp`](images/dos-ega-free-throw-lineup.webp) | MS-DOS (EGA, 16 colours) | Scoreboard, scrolling half-court view, hoop in 3/4 perspective, free-throw lane line-up |
| [`genesis-tipoff.webp`](images/genesis-tipoff.webp) | Sega Genesis | Opening tip-off, centre-court view, scoreboard, benches, scorer's table |
| [`genesis-gameplay-half-court.webp`](images/genesis-gameplay-half-court.webp) | Sega Genesis | Play at the basket, shot clock on the stanchion, bench, oblique end-of-court perspective |
| [`later-ea-game-target-look.png`](images/later-ea-game-target-look.png) | *Bulls versus Blazers and the NBA Playoffs* (EA, 1992 — as identified by the project owner) | The look NtBA is aiming for: tight half-court camera, large animated players, subtle floor, centre logo, courtside ad boards, compact HUD |
