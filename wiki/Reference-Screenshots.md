# Reference screenshots

Reference-only screenshots of the 1989 DOS and 1990 Genesis versions of *Lakers versus Celtics and
the NBA Playoffs* (© Electronic Arts) and of *TV Sports: Basketball* (© Cinemaware). They are here so contributors can study the look of the
original; they are **not** covered by this project's GPL license and **must not** be used as game
assets (NtBA's art is original). If a rights holder asks, they will be removed.

| File | Version | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`dos-ega-free-throw-lineup.webp`](images/dos-ega-free-throw-lineup.webp) | MS-DOS (EGA, 16 colours) | Scoreboard, scrolling half-court view, hoop in 3/4 perspective, free-throw lane line-up |
| [`genesis-tipoff.webp`](images/genesis-tipoff.webp) | Sega Genesis | Opening tip-off, centre-court view, scoreboard, benches, scorer's table |
| [`genesis-gameplay-half-court.webp`](images/genesis-gameplay-half-court.webp) | Sega Genesis | Play at the basket, shot clock on the stanchion, bench, oblique end-of-court perspective |
| [`later-ea-game-target-look.png`](images/later-ea-game-target-look.png) | *Bulls versus Blazers and the NBA Playoffs* (EA, 1992 — as identified by the project owner) | The look NtBA is aiming for: tight half-court camera, large animated players, subtle floor, centre logo, courtside ad boards, compact HUD |
| [`genesis-lakers-celtics-title.png`](images/genesis-lakers-celtics-title.png) | Sega Genesis | Title screen: black background, big stacked outlined team-name lettering around a small half-court-and-crowd picture, twinkling star sparkles in the corners, "and the NBA Playoffs" line, small yellow credit text at the bottom |
| [`tvsports-basketball-title.png`](images/tvsports-basketball-title.png) | *TV Sports: Basketball* (Cinemaware, 1990 — as identified by the project owner) | Title screen: a framed, bevelled bronze plaque with a big winged logotype and a basketball over a blurred arena and crowd, a small publisher badge at the top and a decorative border around the screen |
| [`tvsports-basketball-mode-menu.png`](images/tvsports-basketball-mode-menu.png) | *TV Sports: Basketball* (Cinemaware, 1990 — as identified by the project owner) | Play-mode menu: three chunky outlined orange items (Exhibition, League Play, Clipboard) on a plain textured dark-teal background, a small basketball as the selection cursor, a tiny copyright line at the bottom |
