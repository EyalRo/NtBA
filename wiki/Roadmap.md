# Roadmap

The **plans and ideas live in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues)** so
anyone can comment, vote with a 👍, or pick one up. The checklist version is the
[Roadmap overview](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues/34) tracking issue. This page only gives the
big picture.

## Milestones

| Stage | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **POC** | One team, no opposition. Run, pass, shoot, score. Shot clock, game clock. Playable in the browser. Period-style look with a scrolling side view and an end-on half-court view. | Done |
| **MVP** | Single-player versus a bot: five-on-five with defence, steals/blocks, hold-and-release shooting with fakes, fouls and free throws, the main violations, periods and overtime, sound. | [Issues labelled `milestone: MVP`](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3A%22milestone%3A+MVP%22) |
| **Players & modes** | Player skills and positions, two local players, 1-on-1 and other team sizes, create-a-player. | [`milestone: players-and-modes`](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3A%22milestone%3A+players-and-modes%22) |
| **Online** | Peer-to-peer play between two humans, built on the deterministic input-per-tick core, with an online lobby, peer-to-peer chat and challenges (done; see [Online Play](Online-Play)). Open: cross-browser (Firefox, Safari) and cross-network verification, the TURN relay decision, rollback, spectators. | [`milestone: online`](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3A%22milestone%3A+online%22) |
| **Retro modes & polish** | 1984 mode (graphics and sound), hand-drawn sprites, TV presentation, more cameras. | [`milestone: polish`](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3A%22milestone%3A+polish%22) |
| **Desktop** | Native build of the same Rust core. | [`milestone: desktop`](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3A%22milestone%3A+desktop%22) |
