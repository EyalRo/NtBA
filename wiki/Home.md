# NtBA wiki — Not the Basketball Association

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/EyalRo/NtBA/images/box-art.jpg" alt="NtBA box art" width="320"></p>

Welcome! NtBA is an open-source retro basketball game that you can
[play in your browser](https://eyalro.github.io/NtBA/), free and with nothing to install. It is a love
letter to the golden age of home-computer basketball, built in the open so **you can help shape it**.

**New here?** Start with [Get Involved](Get-Involved). Want a look first? See the
[Screenshots](Screenshots).

![NtBA gameplay, side view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/EyalRo/NtBA/images/game-side-view.png)
