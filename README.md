Remember when sports games came in a big cardboard box, the court was made of chunky pixels, and
the scoreboard glowed amber? **NtBA** brings that feeling back. Lace up, dribble down the floor
under the arena lights, and sink one while the pixel crowd goes wild.

It's a love letter to the golden age of home-computer basketball — and it's built in the open, so
**you can help shape it.**

![NtBA end-on half-court view](assets/screenshot-end-view.png)

## What you can do today

- 🏀 **Two players, one keyboard** — five-on-five, each of you steers one player and the AI runs the
  other four, against the shot clock and the game clock
- 🛡️ **Real defence** — run into the ball handler to strip the ball, swipe for steals, block shots,
  jump passing lanes
- 🎥 **Two camera angles** — a sideline view for the break, and an end-on view when you attack the hoop
- 🎯 **Smart shooting** — long shots are riskier, threes are worth the gamble
- 🏟️ **Arena atmosphere** — a packed crowd, benches, referees and a glowing scoreboard
- 🖥️ **Plays right in your browser** — nothing to install

> **Early days.** This is a playable prototype. A computer opponent is next, so for now bring a
> friend.

## How to play

| Home (gold) | Away (green) | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WASD** | Arrow keys | Run (on the end-on view, **Up** runs toward the basket) |
| **X** | **K** | Shoot — on defence, swipe for a steal or jump to block |
| **Z** | **J** | Pass — hold a direction to pick your target |
| **C** | **L** | Switch to the teammate nearest the ball |
| **Enter** | | Play again after the final buzzer |

On defence, run into the ball handler to steal the ball. Lost your player off screen? An arrow in
your shirt colour on the edge of the screen points the way.

Click the game if the keys don't respond.

## Where we're headed

Beating a computer-controlled team. Playing a friend online. 1-on-1
street ball. Players with real skills — shooters, passers, thieves — and positions like forward
and center. A **1984 mode** with chunky early-80s graphics and chip-tune sound. Broadcast-style
presentation. And much more.

Everything planned, and every idea anyone has had, lives in the
**[issue tracker](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues)**. Browse it, 👍 what you'd like to see
first, or add your own.

## Join the team

NtBA is built by people who love this stuff — and there's room for you, whatever you do:

- 💡 **Got an idea?** [Add it here](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues/new/choose) — no idea is too small or too wild.
- 🎨 **Pixel artist?** Original sprites are the most wanted contribution.
- 🎵 **Make music or sound effects?** The crowd needs a voice.
- 🧑‍💻 **Programmer?** Pick an issue marked [good first issue](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22).
- 🏀 **Just a fan?** Play it and tell us what feels right and what doesn't.

Start at the **[Wiki](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/wiki)** — it has the vision, the roadmap, and
a [guide to getting involved](https://github.com/EyalRo/NtBA/wiki/Get-Involved).
